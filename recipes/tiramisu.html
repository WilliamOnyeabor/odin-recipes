Tiramisu

Tiramisu is a popular coffee-flavored Italian dessert that originated in the Veneto region of Italy. This recipie serves 4.

  • Eggs, 4
  • Mascarpone cheese, 500g
  • Ladyfinger biscuits, 175g (enough to cover the bottom of the dish/dishes you are using)
  • Coffee, 100g
  • Sugar, 100g
  1. To prepare the Tiramisu, first brew the coffee. Filter, aeropress or esspresso is preferred but any strong coffee will work. Let it cool in a shallow wide bowl, in the fridge, whilst you prepare the Tiramisu.
  2. Separate the eggs, dividing the whites from the yolks; remember that to whisk the whites properly there must be no trace of yolk. Add half the amount of sugar to the yolks and beat with an electric whisk until the mixture becomes pale and frothy (this may take 2-3 minutes).
  3. With the beaters still running, add the mascarpone little by little until you obtain a dense, compact cream.
  4. Clean the whisk, thoroughly, add the remaining sugar to the egg whites, then whisk until they can form stiff peaks (this may take a few minutes).
  5. Take a spoonful of the beaten whites and pour it into the bowl with the mascarpone cream and mix with a spatula.
  6. Then add the remaining whites all at once, folding very gently from the bottom to the top . The mascarpone cream is now ready.
  7. The Tiramisu can be prepared in individual dishes or in one large dish. If using individual dishes; dip both sides of a ladyfinger in the coffee and place on the bottom of the dish. Repeat two/three times until the dish is covered (or nearly covered). Add a layer of mascapone then shake the dish to allow the cream to get into the gaps between the ladyfingers. Add a small amount of chocolate powder on the top.
